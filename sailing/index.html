Harbour Buoy Race

Race across Sydney Harbour: round Shark Island, Bradleys Head and Fort Denison in order, then head back east to the finish. Wind blows from the top of the chart (north) and shifts as you go.

← →Steer (or Port / Stbd buttons) ↑ / ↓Trim in / ease out the sail

You can't sail within 40° of the wind (the shaded wedge). To reach the top mark, zigzag upwind by tacking. Keep the trim needle in the green band, and keep off the shore: running aground stops you dead.
