RSYS Harbour Race

Sail the Royal Sydney Yacht Squadron Saturday courses on Sydney Harbour. Pick your division and the breeze, and the race committee signals the course from the sailing instructions.

← →Steer (or Port / Stbd buttons) ↑ / ↓Trim in / ease out the sail Drag · scrollLook around the course (pinch or + / − to zoom, C to recenter) MSound on / off

Cross the start line, then round every mark in order: green marks are left to starboard, red marks to port. You can't sail within 30° of the wind (the shaded wedge), so tack upwind. The breeze also pushes you sideways, most when you are slow or close-hauled, so give marks and the shore some room. Keep the trim needle in the green band and keep off the shore: running aground stops you dead.

Unofficial fan game. Courses are from the RSYS 2026-2027 Spring & Summer Series sailing instructions; mark, start and finish positions are approximate.
